# Standard Questions

## Preamble - read this before starting! 

Before attempting your project, research and answer these questions provided, as a deliverable in a document. Answering these questions should teach you background knowledge. Your responses will not be marked - this is an exercise to help you learn, so producing an "impressive" answer is counterproductive! Many of these questions do not have a right answer. There is no requirement to answer all of these questions, but attempting them will aid your decision of project and be a good learning opportunity.

Using Large Language Models to simply generate answers for these may be detrimental to your ability to solve the problems you will encounter later. Many of these are based on technical questions you might see in an internship interview, so being able to give an honest, unaided answer might be helpful later!

## Questions

1. What is a CubeSat? What are some fundamental components of designing for space that should be considered?

2. What guides or shapes engineering process or design?

3. You are planning a small design project. How will you break down the work?

4. You have been given a small cube-shaped payload to accommodate on a CubeSat by designing a small part. What information would you want to know before designing this part? 
 
5. A wind turbine’s power output has been steadily declining for a period of time. What might be the reasons why? 

6. You have to choose between steel, aluminium, and titanium for a structural component. What questions would you ask to make that choice? 

7. What kind of design features might make a part difficult or impossible to manufacture or assemble?

8. You are designing a deployment mechanism for a solar panel on a satellite. It needs to move smoothly, but the mechanism gets stuck in testing. What might be causing this problem, and what tests might you perform?     

9. What elements of the onboarding project you're interested in do you think might be hard? How might you want to handle them?
